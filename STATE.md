# State

**Last updated:** 2026-10-23
**Phase:** 1 of the roadmap (general scope and details). The owner has started building the site's look

## What exists
- The project docs: `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `STATE.md`, and `ROADMAP.md`.
- The folder layout: `frontend/`, `backend/`, `data/` (`courses/`, `careers/`), and `docs/` (`data-sources/`), and `research/`. Each folder has a README that explains its purpose.
- `frontend/`: a React + Vite app with a header: the blue CourseMap logo (64px wide, hanging left of a 44px box so the title and header height stay put), the title CourseMap next to it, and a white pill-shaped bar of 8 tabs centered on the header, with a light shadow so it looks raised: "Map", "Courses", "Careers", "Skill Groupings", "ACM", "Source Analysis", "Course List", and "Dashboard". The first tab, the page the site opens on, was named "Dashboard" until it was renamed "Map"; "Dashboard" is now a separate last tab. Each tab is as wide as its name, and the bar fits around them. Clicking a tab gives it a gray pill background. On Map (the page the site opens on), the header's bottom line is hidden and the logo and title sit in their own pill-shaped outline, with the same light shadow. Below them, the Map now defaults to the Parallel paths 2D design preview (`ParallelPaths.jsx`): six sample courses on the left, four illustrative career paths on the right, and curved links between them. Clicking a course or career path highlights its sample connections in blue; clicking a course also shows the existing Course Catalog sidebar. Labels stay visible, and a caption makes clear that all links are illustrative. A button fixed at the bottom left switches between Parallel paths and the existing 3D graph. On narrow screens, the Map tabs wrap and the Course Catalog sidebar stacks above the graph. The design is still in progress, and no course-to-career logic has been decided. In the retained 3D view, the placeholder course map works as before: the 109 courses are spread through an oval-shaped 3D space and drawn with perspective, so farther nodes and lines are smaller, fade toward white, and sit behind nearer ones. A soft oval shadow sits a little below the model's lowest node, as wide as the model, and fades out while the model is being turned. It opens zoomed out (about 750×530px on screen) and slightly turned so the depth shows. Dragging turns the model (side to side, and tipping toward or away from the viewer). Scrolling or pinching zooms in and out around the mouse (0.3× to 3×), which is also how users move around in x and y. Each course is a flat hollow ring in the logo's blue, joined by lighter blue lines. The lines are placeholders only, linking each course to its 2 nearest neighbors in the model, since how courses connect isn't decided yet. The map drifts gently, with nodes wandering slightly in all three directions and lines bowing, and nodes near the mouse lean toward it. Hovering a node shows the course number and name in a pill above it (kept inside the map area), turns the node and its links to Logo 2's purple gradient (the node with a soft glow), and slows all motion to a stop until the mouse leaves. Hovering also opens a white panel (rounded corners, 300×380px) in the top right corner of the map showing the course number and name; the rest of the panel is blank for now. Clicking a node selects it and shows the header's bottom line and the Course Catalog sidebar, the same as picking Map from the menu. The selected node and its links stay highlighted in the purple gradient after the mouse leaves, until another node is clicked, the same node is clicked again, or an empty spot on the map is clicked. With the system's reduce-motion setting on, the map stays still. There, the logo and title are a button: clicking it opens a dropdown menu, as wide as the button, listing the same 8 tabs as the tab bar (clicking outside it or pressing Escape closes it). While the logo and title are in their pill, the menu extends the pill downward as one shape, sliding smoothly out of it on opening and back up into it on closing (instant with the system's reduce-motion setting), with a little space between the pill and the first option. Options turn light gray on hover, and the page that's showing sits in a soft blue pill. Each option works like its tab, except Map, which also brings back the header's bottom line (dropping the pill outline) and shows the Course Catalog sidebar. Picking a tab from the tab bar resets it. Apart from that, the Course List tab has the left sidebar, 340px wide, titled "Course Catalog". Under the title is a white pill-shaped search box, the same one as on the Courses page (look only, no filtering yet). When a course is selected on the map, its course number and name appear under the search box as a subheading. Course List splits the main area into two halves: every course on the left and every career on the right, grouped by source, with no connection between them yet. The Careers tab is a page of just the careers, each its own pill, four to a row, grouped by source, laid out like the Courses page, under the same search bar (placeholder "Search for a career...", look only) and the same filter button, whose dropdown has one set, Source (the four career lists). The Courses tab is a page of just the courses, with no sidebar. Under the header is a light blue bar holding a white pill-shaped search box (magnifying glass on the left, placeholder "Search for a course..."). The search box is visual only and doesn't filter yet. At the bar's far right, outside the search box, is a filter button, a white rounded square outlined like the search box (three equal thin lines, each with a small hollow ring at a different point along it, like sliders), 32px and in the logo's blue, a darker blue on hover, that opens a dropdown of checkboxes: Level (100-, 200-, 300-, 600-, and 700-level) and Source (the four course groups), plus a Clear filters button. These do narrow the courses shown: nothing checked in a set means no narrowing from it, the counts update, groups left empty are hidden, and "No courses match these filters." shows if nothing is left. While any filter is on, a small blue badge on the square's corner counts them. Clicking outside the dropdown or pressing Escape closes it. Below that, each course is its own pill-shaped box, four to a row, with the title in the top left and the course number in the bottom right, grouped by source. The Source Analysis tab has an empty page. The ACM and Skill Groupings pages test a Rondesignlab-inspired style that the rest of the site doesn't use yet, written up as rules in `docs/rondeDesignRules.md`. The two share the style's base (the frosted-glass background, tabs, and header), each with its own layout of glows. The Skill Groupings page mirrors the Dashboard tab in that style: it shows the Dashboard's own Student Stats side panel and folded map, with the same placeholders and interactions, restyled. The side panel is a solid white tile standing off the glass, its sections divided by hairlines instead of gray bars, the year in a black pill, the hours gauge drawn in black with ticks and a dotted inner arc, the year rows on pale fills, and the requirement squares black when met and pale when not, with white hover panels. The folded map loses its outline and creases: its blank panels are solid white tiles (the first on the right is black), and the small map between them stands alone, with black lines in pale channels and black hubs with white pins (CSC 111, the intro class, a hollow ring). The tiles rise in one after another, the gauge sweeps in, and the map draws in from the top, each node appearing as the lines reach it, all still with reduce motion. On phones the side panel's tile sits above the map. On ACM, the whole window is a light glowing behind a pane of frosted glass: a narrow column of warm glows (amber, coral, and gold, with white centers) on an off-white base, and two dark spots beside it, which the glass blurs, with a pale sheen and faint streaks; the light drifts slowly. The header has no bottom line, and each tab is its own pill with a thin black outline and light black text; hovering shades a tab lightly and pressing one sinks it in a little. The page below the header splits into a top half and a bottom half. The four panels fill the top right quarter, and the gauge sits in the middle of the top left quarter, halfway between the window's left edge and the panels, growing with the window but staying well inside its quarter. The gauge stands on its own, mostly black: fine ticks around a thick channel (black where the fill has passed, pale after), filled solid black up to the needle with a white dot at the fill's end, a fine dotted arc inside, and a needle with a short tail behind a black hub with a white pin, like the map's hollow nodes. The panels are in a square, solid white with rounded corners and a soft shadow; hovering one lifts it a little. Each holds one placeholder stat: a muted label, the value large (out of its total, smaller, where it has one), and a small black picture of it on a pale track with a note under it. Classes taken: 11, with a dot per class (7 filled for the main route, 4 hollow for the branches) and a key. Credit hours: 80 / 120, with a meter and "40 hours to go". Major requirements: 7 / 12, with a square per requirement and "5 to go". 300-level hours: 6 / 12, with a segment per 3-hour class and "CSC 321 and CSC 371". These match the Dashboard's placeholders and the route's classes. Each panel keeps its share of the space, and on very short windows the notes give way. In the bottom half, a black panel, shaped and shadowed like the white ones, fills the first 35%: the student's year ("Junior", year 3 of 4) in white over a column chart of hours by year (32, 32, 16, 0), with thin columns rising from a hairline, each with its hours on top and its year below; the current year's column is white and the rest gray. The student's route fills the other 65%, standing on its own like the gauge. The main route is a black line in a pale channel winding through a node for each core class taken, in order, starting from the intro class: for the placeholder junior, CSC 111, 112, 201, 250, 231, 251, and 280 (the classes the Dashboard marks complete). Classes branch off the class they followed on thinner lines with smaller nodes, leaving the route square to it and bending into each class: CSC 195 off CSC 112, CSC 270 then CSC 371 off CSC 250, and CSC 321 off CSC 251. Each node is a black hub with a white pin like the gauge's, except the first, a hollow ring like the map's nodes, and the latest on the main route, which has a ring slowly pulsing out from it. A dotted line fades out past the latest class, since the route keeps going. Each class's number sits beside its node on the side away from the lines, and hovering or focusing a node gives it a soft halo and shows the class's name in a white pill on the other side. When the page opens, the gauge's fill sweeps out from the left with the needle, the panels rise into place one after another, and the route draws in from the start, each node appearing as the lines reach it. The gauge doesn't measure anything, and the stats and classes are placeholders. With the system's reduce-motion setting on, nothing moves. On narrower screens, the tabs wrap under the brand. On phones everything stacks (the gauge, the panels, the black panel, then the route), and the route runs down the middle of the page with each core class's number and name to its right and the branches off to the left. The Dashboard tab has a 340px left sidebar titled "Student Stats", with the student's year ("Junior") in a soft blue pill and three sections, each under a gray header bar that folds it open or closed: Classes Taken (a list of 4 placeholder classes, course number above the name), Total Hours (a half-circle gauge where a full gauge is 120 hours: a pale blue track filled soft blue up to the total, a blue needle with a hollow hub like the map's nodes pointing at it, 0 and 120 at the ends, and the total under the hub; then a rounded panel per year, First Year through Senior, with the year on the left and that year's hours on the right), and Major Requirements (a calendar-style grid of 12 small rounded squares, one per requirement: filled soft blue when complete, a much paler blue with a soft blue outline when not, with "7 of 12 requirements met" under it). The placeholder requirements are 11 specific classes plus a number of 300-level hours (6 of 12). Hovering or focusing a square outlines it and opens a white panel under its row, pointing up at it (or over the row, pointing down, when there isn't room below it in the window), with the requirement, its course name or hours, and whether it's complete (in words, beside a small matching square). All values are placeholders until there's sign-in. The main area is a folded map in three sections: two blank panels on the left, a small static map of 7 placeholder nodes joined by 9 lines in the middle, drawn like the course map (hollow blue rings, lighter blue lines), and three blank panels on the right. Each node has a sample class (CSC 111, 112, 201, 231, 250, 301, 331), and hovering a node shows its course number and name in a pill above it, like the course map's, kept inside the fold. The classes and links are placeholders. In the header's top right corner, on every page except Map, is a round login icon (a person outline in a 40px circle). Clicking it opens a blank page titled "Login" with no tab highlighted. There's no actual sign-in; the icon and page are the look only. Nothing else is built.
- `docs/rondeDesignRules.md`: design rules drawn from the ACM page's style (background, color, layout, surfaces, type, data visualization, nodes and routes, motion, accessibility, and what was tried and rejected), so other pages and elements can match it without copying the page.
- `research/`: PDFs with sources at the top. Two are lists from the first data pull on 2026-10-21 (titles only), and one covers the tech stack.
  - `wfu-cs-courses.pdf`: 42 undergraduate courses, 41 graduate courses, and 26 special-topics offerings. Sources: the 2026-2027 WFU bulletins and the CS department site.
  - `cs-careers.pdf`: GSU Pathways (94 titles), BLS OOH (10), O\*NET Computer and Mathematical family (38), and NICE Framework v2.2.0 work roles (42).
  - `tech-stack.pdf`: the project's stack, with a brief reason for each choice.
- `data/`: `courses/courses.json` (109 courses) and `careers/careers.json` (184 careers), the same lists as the research PDFs, grouped by source. The frontend reads them directly until there's a backend.
- Logo assets: `frontend/src/assets/branding/logo-1.svg` (blue) and `logo-2.svg` (purple gradient), scalable versions of the selected folded-map/node design. The approved image is preserved alongside them as `approved-reference.png`. The header currently uses Logo 1.
- No backend code yet. Git is handled by another agent.
